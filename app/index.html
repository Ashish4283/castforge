    Build a prop from a reference image
    
      
    Upload a clear prop photo, choose the closest editable 3D prop type, and set its real dimensions and material. Blender builds the model locally and keeps your image beside it as a non-rendering reference for further editing. The image does not automatically create textures or hidden geometry.
